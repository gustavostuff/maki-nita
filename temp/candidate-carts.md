# Candidate carts

Eight cart sketches. The field is 16 by 16 and one bit. The controls are one stick and one action button. The tick is 60 Hz. These sketches are separate from space-shooter, paddle, and arkaled.

## Munch

The model is Pac-Man. A small maze holds a few dots and one ghost. The stick walks the lanes. A lit cell is a dot or the ghost.

## Flap

The model is Joust. The action button is the wing. A meeting scores for the higher rider.

## Cycle

The model is Tron. The stick turns and the trail stays lit. The other rider belongs to the machine.

## Slide

The model is Pengo. The action button shoves a block. The block glides until it hits something.

## Aim

The model is Missile Command. The stick moves a crosshair. The action button fires at falling sparks.

## Gem

The model is Boulder Dash. The stick digs. A boulder falls when the cell under it goes dark.

## Grill

The model is BurgerTime. The figure walks across a bun, a patty, and lettuce. The action button shakes pepper at the chaser.

## Drift

The model is Asteroids. Left and right turn the ship. Up is thrust. The action button fires. Rocks wrap around the field.
