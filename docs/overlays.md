# Screen overlays

The field is 16x16 and 1-bit. Color and scenery come from a physical sheet in front of white or neutral-white matrices, the same idea as Magnavox Odyssey overlays on a CRT.

The sheet is part of the normal design alongside cartridges. Firmware stays 1-bit. A title runs with no sheet installed.

The playfield uses white light only. A black mask with translucent colored windows filters that light. Single-color matrix parts cannot carry color in plastic.

Sheets can ship with a cart, or be cut from a shared template. Swapping a sheet is a physical step beside inserting a cart. The overlays doc does not fix case dimensions or faceplate mechanics. Those stay outside KiCad until they are drawn there.
